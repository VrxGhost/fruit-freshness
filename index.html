How it works, results and limitations

How it works

  • A MobileNetV2 network pretrained on ImageNet was fine-tuned in PyTorch on the Kaggle dataset Fruits fresh and rotten for classification (6 classes).
  • The dataset's 13,599 images come from only 1,511 photographs, and its provided test split was 100% leaked, so the data was re-split by original photograph.
  • The trained model was exported to ONNX and runs here with ONNX Runtime Web. No server is involved.
  • Predictions below 70% confidence are reported as uncertain and should be checked by a person.

Results on the leak-free test set (2,007 images)

ModelAccuracyMacro F1
MobileNetV2 (transfer learning)99.50%0.9947

Rotten-fruit recall: 99.65%. No fresh fruit was wrongly rejected.

Limitations

  • Trained only on apples, bananas and oranges, one fruit per photo.
  • Any photo, even of a non-fruit object, is forced into one of the six classes; the 70% threshold reduces but does not remove this.
  • Training images are clean close-ups, so real-world photos with clutter or poor light may score lower.
